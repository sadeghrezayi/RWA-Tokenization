| 2026-08-11 | Chain writes on the single operator key — CLOSED | Two separate defects, now both fixed. **(1) Stale cached nonce:** the ONCHAINID claim issuer held one ethers NonceManager for the life of the process while every other adapter took one per operation. Sends are now serialised per account: ONLY a promise lane is shared, while each send still gets its own manager. Sharing a NonceManager instead was tried twice and reverted — it allocates optimistically, so a send that never reaches the chain leaves a gap and everything behind it queues forever (measured: a chain suite hung 900s). Forcing a nonce re-read per send is also wrong: it hits ethers' 250ms RPC cache and the second of two rapid sends comes back "nonce too low". Both properties are now integration-tested (concurrent senders get distinct nonces; a failed send does not block the queue) and the full suite runs clean in ~65s across three consecutive runs. **Process note:** three earlier fixes were built on an unverified hypothesis; what actually resolved it was making the failure legible — logging 5xx that arrive as HttpException, and trimming the log attached to CI failures so the annotation stopped truncating it |
